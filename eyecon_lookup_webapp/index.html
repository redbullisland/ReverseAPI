    Where do I get the Client ID?
    
    The server rejects unknown IDs with 401. The app stores it as the clientId preference after phone verification, and sends it as the e-auth header. Options:
    
    
      
      
    • Capture a real request from the app with mitmproxy/Charles and copy the e-auth header value.
      • 
      
    • On a rooted device or emulator, read the app's shared_prefs for clientId.
      • 
      
    • Hook Authenticator.m16803d() with Frida and print the return value.
      • 
    
    
    
    The e-auth-c checksum and e-auth-k key are computed automatically from the ID.
